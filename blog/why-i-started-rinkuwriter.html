My Writing Journey

Why I Started RinkuWriter

Ek chhoti si shuruaat, ek naye safar ki taraf.
September 25, 2026 • 3 min read • Rinku

Har kisi ke paas kehne ke liye kuch na kuch hota hai. Kabhi woh ek thought hota hai, kabhi koi experience, kabhi koi dream aur kabhi bas ek aisi feeling jise words dena mushkil hota hai.

RinkuWriter mere liye sirf ek website nahi hai. Yeh unhi thoughts, ideas aur experiences ko words mein convert karne ki ek chhoti si koshish hai.

Maine yeh journey kisi perfect plan ke saath start nahi ki. Bas ek simple thought tha — jo kuch main seekh raha hoon, soch raha hoon aur experience kar raha hoon, usse likhna chahiye.

"Har badi journey ki shuruaat ek chhote step se hoti hai."

Writing ki sabse interesting baat mujhe yeh lagti hai ki ek simple idea bhi jab words mein aata hai, toh woh kisi aur ke liye inspiration, knowledge ya ek naya perspective ban sakta hai.

Isi thought ke saath maine RinkuWriter ko start kiya. Main chahta hoon ki yeh ek aisi jagah bane jahan mere thoughts sirf mere tak limited na rahen, balki un logon tak bhi pahunch sakein jo unhe padhna chahte hain.

The Beginning

This website is not the destination. It is the beginning of the journey.

RinkuWriter par main apne articles, thoughts, stories, experiences aur future mein apni books se judi cheezein share karna chahta hoon.

Yeh sirf writing ka platform nahi hoga. Yeh meri learning aur growth ki journey ka bhi ek record hoga. Main yahan likhunga, seekhunga, galtiyan karunga, improve karunga aur apni journey ko document karta rahunga.

Shayad aaj yahan bahut kuch na ho. Shayad yeh beginning simple ho. Lekin mujhe lagta hai ki kisi bhi bade dream ko reality mein badalne ke liye sabse pehle ek step lena padta hai.

Aur RinkuWriter mera woh pehla step hai.

Agar tum yahan tak aaye ho, toh thank you. Tumne meri journey ke first page ko padha hai.

Ho sakta hai future mein tum yahan meri koi story padho, koi thought tumhare mind mein reh jaaye, ya kisi article se tumhe kuch naya seekhne ko mile.

Main nahi jaanta ki yeh journey mujhe exactly kahan le jayegi. Lekin ek cheez zaroor pata hai — main ise continue karna chahta hoon.

This is just the beginning.

— Rinku Founder & Writer at RinkuWriter
